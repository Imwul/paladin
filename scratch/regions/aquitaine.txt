Territories
The immense archduchy, and later sub-kingdom, of 
Aquitaine roughly stretches from the Atlantic coast between 
Bordeaux and Nantes to the Loire River in the east and Garonne 
River in the south. It is divided into four important sub-duchies: 
Auvergne, Guyenne, Limousin and Poitou, each of which are 
themselves divided into counties.
Though potentially one of the richest areas of the realm, 
the Aquitainian countryside has been ravaged by a decade of 
warfare and plundering. The land is completely ravaged after the 
conquest of Aquitaine in 769, unlike Gascony, which submitted 
peacefully. Pepin the Short destroyed most city walls and laid 
waste to enormous swathes of land. The population has suffered 
immensely, and this once-prosperous region with its rich Roman 
towns is now only sparsely populated, as many inhabitants have 
fled or been killed.
The lands south of the Loire River have remained a 
world apart from the Frankish heartlands. The proud and in -
dependent-minded Gascons were never really subdued by the 
conquests of Charles Martel and Pepin the Short. Therefore 
Franks are very suspicious of these “Romans.” Even during 
Charlemagne’s reign, Aquitaine remains apart until Louis is 
made their sub-king, even though most counts are Franks, rath-
er than Gascons.
Most of Aquitaine is rolling foothills and river plains. These 
once-fertile hills and valleys used to be covered with vineyards, 
but have been laid waste by the long and bitter war pitting the 
Bordelais against the Lorrainers and the Frankish mayors and 
kings. The country presents no major travel difficulties, and the 
climate is less rough than in the north, with warm summers and 
mild winters.
Duchy of Auvergne
Rulers: Duke Aubery the Burgundian (765–773); Duke 
Berthmond (774–778); Duke Ithier (778–790); and Duke 
Robert the Burgundian (790–819). This is part of King 
Carloman’s domain from 768 to 771.
Regional Modifiers: The inhabitants of the volcanic 
hills of Auvergne are a taciturn, hospitable, headstrong and 
hard-working people. They receive a +1d3 bonus to Energetic, 
Generous, Honest and Suspicious.
