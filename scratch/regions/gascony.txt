Regional Modifiers: Like the folks from Aquitaine, the 
Gascons are known as an elegant but slightly decadent people. 
They receive +1d3 to Deceitful, Arbitrary, and 3 courtly skills 
of their choice.
Territory
The duchy of Gascony is limited in the west by the Atlantic 
coast up to Bordeaux in the north, the Garonne River and 
T oulouse in the east and the Pyrenees in the south. The coastal 
plains and rolling inland hills at the foot of the western Pyrenees 
are only thinly inhabited. The climate is clement, with mild win-
ters and hot summers.
