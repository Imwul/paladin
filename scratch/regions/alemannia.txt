Territory
Also called the duchy of Swabia, the Alemannian territory 
lies west of Bavaria, centered around Lake Constance. It extends 
from the northern Alps along the west bank of the Lech River up 
to the region around Augsburg. The northern boundary roughly 
leads from Augsburg in the east along the Black Forest in the 
west down to Basel. The duchy is divided into 21 small gau; the 
local name for a county, as in Thurgau or Rheingau.
Alemannia is a very mountainous region, with steep, 
heavily forested hills. T ravel is mostly done by boat across the 
many navigable rivers and lakes, of which the Rhine and Lake 
Constance are most important. The duchy has cold winters and 
warm summers.
