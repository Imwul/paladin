ulture: Visigothic.
Ruler: Duke Milo of Aiglent (760–771); Duke Chorso 
of Aquitaine (778–790); Duke William Shortnose 
(790–806); Duke Bego of Paris (806–826). From 768 to 771 
this area is nominally under the rule of King Carloman. And 
from 771 to 778, Septimania has no duke and is governed di -
rectly from the royal court of King Charlemagne.
Regional Modifiers: Septimanian nobles, exuberant 
and loyal, are notoriously boastful and hot-headed. They re -
ceive +1d3 to Proud, Reckless, V alorous, Love [family], Hate 
[Moors], and Languages.
Territory
Septimania is relatively urbanized. This Romanized and 
Visigothic region covers the area of the Aude River basin, 
north of the Pyrenees and south of the Black Mountains, from 
T oulouse and the eastern banks of Garonne River up to the 
Rhône River in the west. Behind the dunes lie many coastal la -
goons backed by arid craggy hills. The inland is mostly fertile 
river plains, surrounded by gentle foothills.
