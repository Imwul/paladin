Regional Modifiers: Life in Thuringia is fraught with

plunder and slaughter. Knights from this duchy receive +1d3 to 
V engeful, Arbitrary, Cruel, Prudent, V alorous, Love [God], Folk 
Lore, and may choose to gain +5 Hate [Saxons].
Territory
Thuringia is the small southern part of Saxony that King 
Pepin conquered, and as such it has become a frontier march sit-
uated between the rivers Main, W erra, Unstut and Saale. The 
landscape is a monotonous hilly or mountainous forest, with 
many rivers and streams running through it.
The Franks are still colonizing and converting the region, 
and the Duke of Thuringia is honor-bound to protect Austrasia 
from Saxon and Slav attacks. Once the Franks conquer more 
Saxon territory, Charlemagne creates the duchy of Saxony, 
which incorporates Thuringia, in 782.
